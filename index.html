      Dev panel

      Auto facing: enemy pawns face inward from the nearest edge, player pawns face outward toward it.

      Legend & keys
      • ground (level 0) plateau (1) summit (2)
      • Thick dark line = step up: costs 1 extra movement, so kings, knights and pawns can't climb it. Chevrons = ramp (free climb, points uphill).
      • Blue dashed outline = deployment zone (the hill's base).
      • Enemy intents: numbered arrows in execution order. Red = capture, orange = move, grey dashed = blocked by an earlier enemy move in the sequence (that enemy picks another move). Purple + = gives check, black # = checkmate.
      • Pawn badge = captures; small tick = facing. Hover a pawn to see its promotion. Unmoved pawns may double-step.
      • Enemy pawns spawn only in the middle 4 squares of each edge.
      • Faded red pieces with a dashed box on the edge = enemies arriving at the end of this turn. Standing on a marker pushes that spawn elsewhere on the same edge.
      • Faded piece = already acted this turn. ↻ = can Redeploy.
      • Keys: Enter end turn · Z undo · Esc cancel

      King of the Hill

      Hold the hill. Enemies march in from every edge; their next moves are shown as arrows. Keep your king out of checkmate.

      Score = value of enemies you capture (pawn 1, knight/bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9) + 10 × wave number for each wave you wipe out.
